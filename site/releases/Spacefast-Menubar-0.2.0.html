Spacefast 0.2.0

  • Keyboard everything. Arrows move, ⌘← ⌘→ switch teams, Return opens, ⌘C copies the link. Press ? for the full list.
  • Dark thumbnails in dark mode. Spaces with a dark theme now show it in the grid.
  • Tabs fade at the edges when there are more teams than fit.
  • Updates. The app checks for new versions on its own, or use Check for Updates… in the settings menu.
