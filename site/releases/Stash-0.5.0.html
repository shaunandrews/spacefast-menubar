Stash 0.5.0

  • Opens at login. Stash is there after a restart. Turn it off in the settings menu.
  • Sharper dark thumbnails. They come from Spacefast's screenshot service now, private Spaces included, instead of loading each Space in the background.
  • No more "Generating Preview". Fresh screenshots fill in on their own.
  • Sign out tells you if the key couldn't be revoked.
  • An app icon, so Stash stops looking like a blank app in Finder.
