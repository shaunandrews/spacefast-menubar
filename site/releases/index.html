Release notes

What's new in each version. Stash updates itself; ⚙︎ › Check for Updates… if you can't wait.

0.5.0 November 2, 2026

  • Opens at login. Stash is there after a restart. Turn it off in the settings menu.
  • Sharper dark thumbnails. They come from Spacefast's screenshot service now, private Spaces included, instead of loading each Space in the background.
  • No more "Generating Preview". Fresh screenshots fill in on their own.
  • Sign out tells you if the key couldn't be revoked.
  • An app icon, so Stash stops looking like a blank app in Finder.

0.4.1 November 2, 2026

  • Discover moves up. It's the binoculars tab now, right after Favorites, and ⌘2 jumps to it.
  • Release notes. Every release is listed at stash.space.fast/releases, and ⚙︎ › Release Notes takes you there.

0.4.0 November 2, 2026

  • Discover. A new last tab with the Spaces people are sharing on Spacefast. New ones since your last look get a dot.
  • Remix from the right-click menu, where a Space allows it.
  • Not into it? Turn off Show Discover in the settings menu.

0.3.1 November 2, 2026

  • New home. Stash lives at stash.space.fast now, and checks there for updates.

0.3.0 November 2, 2026

  • Meet Stash. Spacefast's menu bar app has a name. Same app, same Spaces, same shortcuts.

0.2.0

  • Keyboard everything. Arrows move, ⌘← ⌘→ switch teams, Return opens, ⌘C copies the link. Press ? for the full list.
  • Dark thumbnails in dark mode. Spaces with a dark theme now show it in the grid.
  • Tabs fade at the edges when there are more teams than fit.
  • Updates. The app checks for new versions on its own, or use Check for Updates… in the settings menu.

0.1.0 November 2, 2026

  • First release. Your newest Spaces in the menu bar: every team in a tab, a list or a grid of screenshots, favorites, copy a link, and a panel that runs a Space like a mini app.
