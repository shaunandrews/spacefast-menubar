Stash 0.6.0

  • A new look. Stash borrows from spacefast.com now: Spacefast's typeface, a rounded panel, and your tabs in a track with a green pill that slides between them. Light and dark both.
  • Little thumbnails in the list, so you can spot a Space without switching to the grid.
  • Hover actions in one bar. Star, copy the link, or open the dashboard from a single strip that stays readable over any screenshot.
  • Some fun. Cards tip when you pick them up, stars pop when you favorite (and wobble when you don't), copying a link earns a sticker, and your newest Space gets a "fresh" one.
  • Everything up top. Dashboard and settings moved into the header, so the footer's gone and the list gets the room.
